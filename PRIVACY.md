# Privacy Policy for Sublurk

_Last updated: 2026-07-13_

Sublurk ("the app," "we," "us") is a game that runs inside Reddit via Reddit's
Developer Platform (Devvit). This policy explains what data the app processes,
why, and who it is shared with. By using Sublurk you agree to this policy.

## Who this is from

Sublurk is an independent project. For any privacy questions or requests, contact:
**satrajitmondal06@gmail.com**

## What the app processes

Sublurk is a text-driven dungeon game. To run, it processes the following:

- **Actions you type.** When you play, the free-text actions you enter (for
  example, "search the altar" or "attack the guardian") are sent to a
  third-party AI service to generate the story narration for your turn. See
  "Third parties" below.
- **Your Reddit username.** Used to save your private solo run so you can
  resume it, and to record your best solo run on a leaderboard shown to other
  players. Only your username, your deepest depth reached, and the number of
  rolls it took are stored for the leaderboard — one entry per player.
- **Game state.** Your run's progress (character class, hit points, in-game
  currency, inventory, current room, and a log of what has happened in the run)
  is stored so the game can continue and so a run can be resumed.
- **Moderator status.** For community posts, the app checks whether you
  moderate the subreddit in order to show or hide moderator-only controls. This
  check is performed at request time and its result is not stored.
- **Public subreddit content.** For community games, the app reads the host
  subreddit's public name, description, and a sample of its public top post
  titles to generate a dungeon themed to that community. It does not read
  private messages, private posts, or any non-public data.

Sublurk does **not** ask for, collect, or store your email address, real name,
password, IP address, precise location, or payment information. It does not use
advertising or third-party tracking or analytics.

## Where data is stored

Game state, solo run history, and the leaderboard are stored using Reddit's
Developer Platform storage (Redis), operated by Reddit as part of the platform
the app runs on. This data is associated with the subreddit and, for solo runs,
with your Reddit user ID.

## Third parties

- **Google Gemini API.** The actions you type and the current game context are
  sent to Google's Gemini API to generate story narration and to build the game
  world. This data is processed by Google under its own terms and privacy
  policy. Do not type anything into the game that you would not want sent to a
  third-party AI service. Google's privacy policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Reddit.** The app runs on Reddit's Developer Platform and is subject to
  Reddit's Privacy Policy and User Agreement. Reddit's privacy policy:
  https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy

## Data retention and deletion

Game state, solo run history, and leaderboard entries persist so the game can
function (resuming runs, showing standings). A community world and its state can
be cleared by a subreddit moderator using the app's "Reset world" action. If you
would like your solo run data or leaderboard entry removed, contact
**satrajitmondal06@gmail.com** and include your Reddit username.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and is intended for use in
accordance with Reddit's User Agreement, which requires users to be 13 or older.
